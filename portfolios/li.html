About Li – Tattoo Artist at Horimu Studio

Specializes in:

Fine Line Tattoo

Other Styles:

  • Black & Grey Tattoos
  • Color Tattoos

Li is a tattoo artist at Horimu Studio with a strong foundation in fine art and visual composition, specializing in fine line, black & grey, and color tattoo work.

Her work is known for clean execution and detailed design, with color used thoughtfully to enhance each piece.

She focuses on translating abstract ideas into refined, wearable tattoo artwork.
