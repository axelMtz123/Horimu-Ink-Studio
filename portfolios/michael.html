About Michael – Sak Yant Tattoo Artist in Houston

Specializes in:

Thai Hand-Poked Tattoos (Sak Yant)

Michael specializes in traditional Thai hand-poked tattooing (Sak Yant), focusing on authentic technique, precision, and cultural respect.

Each tattoo is created without machines, using hand-poked methods that produce a distinct organic texture and rhythm unique to Sak Yant work.

His approach prioritizes meaning and structure, ensuring each symbol carries both visual strength and deeper spiritual significance rooted in tradition.
