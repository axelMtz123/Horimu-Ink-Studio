About Sophia – Tattoo Artist at Horimu Ink Studio

Specializes in:

Realism & Cybersigilism

Other Styles:

  • Custom Tattoo Work

Meet Sophia, an artist drawn to the balance between raw realism and futuristic distortion. Her work blends detailed black & grey realism with cybersigil-inspired elements, creating pieces that feel both human and otherworldly.

Her style continues to evolve through inspiration from digital culture, music, and contemporary cyber aesthetics while maintaining strong technical detail at the core of every tattoo.
