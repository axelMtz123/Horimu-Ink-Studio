About Isaiah – Tattoo Artist at Horimu Studio

Specializes in:

Black & Grey Tattoo

Other Styles:

  • Anime Tattoos
  • Neo-Traditional Tattoos

Isaiah is a tattoo artist at Horimu Studio specializing in black & grey, anime, and neo-traditional tattoo styles. His work focuses on bold structure, strong contrast, and clean visual composition.

He blends detailed linework with expressive design elements to bring anime and neo-traditional concepts to life with clarity and impact.

His tattoos are designed with long-term visual balance in mind, ensuring readability and strength over time.
